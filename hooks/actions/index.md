# Action Hooks

Action hooks allow you to execute custom code at specific points in Fluent Booking's execution flow. They are perfect for triggering side effects, sending notifications, logging events, or performing custom business logic.

## Categories

1. [Bookings & Scheduling](/hooks/actions/bookings-and-scheduling) - Booking lifecycle, creation, confirmation, cancellation.
2. [Calendars & Events](/hooks/actions/calendars-and-events) - Calendar and event management.
3. [Availability](/hooks/actions/availability) - Availability schedule management.
4. [Integrations](/hooks/actions/integrations) - Third-party integrations and webhooks.
5. [Landing Pages & Display](/hooks/actions/landing-pages-and-display) - Frontend rendering and landing page events.
6. [Admin & Settings](/hooks/actions/admin-and-settings) - Administrative functions and settings.
7. [Payments & Orders](/hooks/actions/payments-and-orders) <Badge type="tip" text="Pro" /> - Payment processing and order management.
